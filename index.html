How this was researched

  1. Started from the U.S. Geological Survey's register of place names, which lists 234 named summits in Hancock County.
  2. Kept the ones whose coordinates fall on Mount Desert Island. Hills on the Cranberry Isles, Tinker Island and the mainland were left out.
  3. Checked each summit's position against the National Park Service boundary for Acadia.
  4. Checked how you reach each top using trail guides, the Land & Garden Preserve map and peakbaggers' trip reports.
  5. Confirmed the edge cases (Bald Mountain, Youngs Mountain and the Redfield Hill trail) on the official park map.

Limits

  • "Named" means named in the federal register. Peakbagging sites list a few more unofficial knobs, such as Bernard's South Peak, that aren't included.
  • Redfield Hill's height isn't published; ≈300 ft is an estimate from its neighbours. The six extra peaks have no published prominence.
  • "Private" means outside the park and with no public trail known. Land changes hands, so check before you go anywhere near a property line.
  • Summits with no maintained trail aren't off limits by law, but the park asks hikers to stay on trails to protect thin soil and summit plants.

Save for offline use

Cell service is patchy on Mount Desert Island. This saves the page and the whole island map to this device, including trails, summits, relief and contours down to trail-level zoom. It's about 25 MB.

  • Once saved, it works with no signal. Open it from your home screen or in the same browser.
  • Only Mount Desert Island is saved. Panning off the island or zooming far out needs a connection.
  • Your checkmarks are always stored on this device and don't need a connection.
  • On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first. Safari can clear saved data for websites you haven't opened in a few weeks.
